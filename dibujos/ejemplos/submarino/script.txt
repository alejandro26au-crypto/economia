You wake up three hundred feet underwater!
Eighty men. One steel tube. Zero windows.
Your bed? Still warm. Another sailor just left it. You share it. Every day.
The air? It's going bad. Light a match... nothing. Not enough oxygen!
Then... BOOM! Depth charges! The enemy is right above you!
The hull groans. The lights die. Nobody moves. Nobody breathes.
And it gets worse. Even the toilet can kill you!
One German sub? Somebody flushed it wrong. Seawater poured in, hit the batteries... and turned into poison gas! Sub lost. Because of a toilet!
One in five American submariners never came home. One in five!
Your only trick? Silence. Go deep. Wait.
Hours later... the bombs stop. You made it!
You crawl into that warm bed. Close your eyes... Ping. They're back. And suddenly...
