You Wake Up in a WWII Submarine

## Descripción
You wake up 300 feet underwater. 80 men, one steel tube, zero windows... and the enemy is right above you. 💣
Could YOU survive a WWII submarine? 👇

Real facts:
• US subs (Gato class) carried about 80 men; crews shared "hot bunks" in shifts.
• Low oxygen meant a match wouldn't even light.
• Seawater + batteries = chlorine gas.
• German U-1206 was lost on April 14, 1945 after a toilet was flushed wrong.
• 52 US submarines and about 3,500 men were lost — roughly 1 in 5 submariners.
